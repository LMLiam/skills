# Decision Packet

Use this format for each consultation.

```markdown
## Decision packet

1. Objective
   [The decision to make.]

2. Constraints
   [Technical, product, safety, cost, or compatibility constraints.]

3. Evidence gathered
   [Observed source, tests, commands, documentation, traces, or logs.]

4. Evidence against the current hypothesis
   [Evidence that would weaken or disprove it. State "None found" only after checking.]

5. Relevant files and symbols
   [Exact paths, symbols, routes, data types, or commands to inspect first.]

6. Current hypothesis
   [The most likely explanation or preferred option.]

7. Options considered
   [Material alternatives and their trade-offs.]

8. Unresolved uncertainty
   [The technical judgement that evidence has not resolved.]

9. Precise question
   [One answerable question for the advisor.]
```

For a completion challenge, also include acceptance criteria, diff scope, verification performed, and known unverified behaviour.
